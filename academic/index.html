About 关于我

I am a master's student at the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences (IIE, CAS), and the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS). My undergraduate studies were in Cyberspace Security at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST).

My research focuses on AI-driven software security analysis and software protection against autonomous reverse engineering agents.

我目前是中国科学院信息工程研究所、中国科学院大学的硕士研究生，本科就读于华中科技大学网络空间安全专业。研究聚焦于人工智能驱动的软件安全分析，以及针对自主逆向分析智能体的软件保护与防御。

Research Interests 研究兴趣

  • Software Security 软件安全

    AI-driven analysis of software behavior and security.人工智能驱动的软件行为与安全分析。

  • AI Security 人工智能安全

    Security of AI systems in software analysis.软件分析场景中的人工智能系统安全。

  • Security of AI Agents 人工智能智能体安全

    Software protection and defenses against autonomous reverse engineering agents.针对自主逆向分析智能体的软件保护与防御。
